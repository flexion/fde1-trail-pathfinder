# Homework 3 retrieval questions

Ten questions derived from the existing `golden.jsonl` test fixtures.
Each runs with the matching scenario context and self-ratings in
[retrieval.jsonl](retrieval.jsonl). These are fixture-derived questions,
not collected user utterances.

1. For the Trail Crew Member role in this scenario, which skills are required, in which categories, and at what levels?

2. Using my self-ratings, which Trail Crew Member requirements do I meet, which am I close to, and which am I missing?

3. Which approved training in this scenario would close my Tread Maintenance - Hand tools gap for Trail Crew Member, what does it cost, and what does completion prove?

4. Is there a free approved training in this scenario for my unrated Drainage Features/Water Management skill, and does it meet the Trail Crew Member requirement?

5. How do the prices and completion evidence differ between Drainage Basics Field Day and Tread Maintenance Intensive?

6. I rated myself Expert in Safety/Site Management under Trail Maintenance. Does that meet this Trail Crew Leader role's Safety/Site Management requirement under Trail Construction?

7. With my Entry rating in Crew Management / Jobsite Safety, which approved course in this scenario meets the Trail Crew Leader requirement, and what does it cost?

8. Which course in this scenario's supplied catalog can close my Trail Designer gaps in Field Design: Trail Alignment Identification and Marking or Corridor Planning?

9. Given my self-ratings for this Trail Crew Member role, do I need the $75 Brushing and Corridor Workshop to meet its requirements?

10. Is Tread Maintenance Intensive available online, and when is its next session?

Coverage: three single-source questions, five combining two sources, and
two declines (8 and 10) where the supplied data cannot answer.
Golden case 6 is excluded until its ranking decision is resolved.

See [the evaluation README](README.md) for source IDs and scoring guidance.
