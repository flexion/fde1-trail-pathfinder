# AI-1 failures: what goes wrong in fifty traces

*Written 2026-11-02 from the fifty traces in [traces.md](traces.md).*

**Source:** 50 runs of `suggest_competencies` (`traces.jsonl`):

- 13 AI-1 golden inputs;
- 34 inputs a wrote across corps, fire, landscaping, veterans, volunteers, trades, office jobs, vague, off-topic and exaggerated answers;
- 3 chip-only inputs.

There are no real user traces yet, because AI-1 has not been in front of anyone. The generated inputs are checked against real people's words at the demo.

**43 of the 50 traces had at least one failure under the first bar.** Line 1 was then dropped (a chip alone may give `likely`), which leaves the lines below it. Five traces declined, all correctly; three of them with the wrong wording (line 10).

**The bar for now: the easy rule** (decided 2026-11-02, until the product owner finalizes).

- A suggested skill **passes** when her words relate to it, including transferable work from another job ("led small teams in the Army" → Crew Management).
- It **fails** when it has no relation to what she wrote, or her words say the opposite ("never had a formal trail job" → Tread Maintenance).
- A skill she described that the output missed is not a fail; she can add it herself.
- A chip alone may give `likely`: the chip matches are right.
- **One verdict per trace.** A trace fails if any pre-checked suggestion (`likely` or `possible`, both ticked on screen) fails. A wrong `check` suggestion alone does not fail it.

The twenty hand scores (`human_scores.jsonl`) use this rule. The 12 that passed the first, stricter scoring are kept; 8 more were picked for re-scoring.

One of those errors was a content-policy flag, possibly from "fire" and "burns". The rerun had none. The traces now record the reason when a fallback happens.

## What went right

Recorded so a scorer is not written for a failure that does not happen:

- Every quote is verbatim from her text.
- Every id is a real framework id.
- No level was raised above Entry.
- Rejected ids are never re-offered (ai1-11).
- The cap of 12 holds (3 traces hit it).
- Every decline decision was right: the five that should decline did (ai1-06, ai1-07, gen-30, gen-31, gen-37; three with the wrong wording, line 10), and nothing else declined. Scorer `declines_when_it_should`.
- Scorer `valid_output` guards the first four of these.

## Where my own criteria wobbled

Lines 6 and 7 involve judgment calls a second reader might make differently:

- Is "clearing brush" Site Preparation or Brushing?
- Is "building rock steps" Hand-Built or Tread Hardening?
- Is "sent emails" enough for Communications?
- gen-31 (retail, "No trail experience yet" chip) passed in the hand scores because nothing maps, though its decline has the same "pick a background" wording that failed gen-30 and gen-37. Thin descriptions make the call hard.

I counted only the cases I would argue for. These are the dimensions to settle with the product owner before a judge is asked to score them.
